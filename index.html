This tool delivers that same level of audit and strategy instantly, on demand — saving users hundreds of dollars monthly and giving them a clear action plan to grow their presence and attract better opportunities.

Podcast to Thread Summarizer

About This Project

An AI-powered automation that takes a podcast episode — audio or transcript — and instantly converts it into a punchy, ready-to-publish Twitter/X thread. It extracts the key insights, structures them into scroll-stopping thread format, and outputs clean copy with hooks and call-to-action — no editing required.

Problem Solved

Podcast creators and thought leaders produce hours of valuable content that never reaches the audiences who prefer short-form platforms. Manually summarising a 60-minute episode into a thread takes 2–3 hours. This tool does it in under 60 seconds — unlocking repurposed content at scale and extending the reach of every episode without extra effort.
